Why The First Business To Respond Wins The Job

4 min read · GrowWise Team

Here is a hard truth about local work. The job usually does not go to the best shop. It goes to the first shop that answers.

This is not a guess. A study in Harvard Business Review found that shops who called a new lead within an hour were about seven times more likely to reach the person in charge. Waiting one more hour cut those odds way down. Other research found the odds drop hard after just five minutes.

Fast answers build trust

Think about it from their side. They called three shops. One texted back in a minute. One called the next morning. One never called at all. Nobody has even talked price yet. But the first shop already proved something. They are on it. If they move that fast before they get paid, they will probably show up on time too.

You cannot fix this by trying harder

Most owners try to answer faster on their own. They take calls on ladders. They text leads at dinner. They feel bad about every voicemail. But you cannot answer every call in five minutes and do the work too. That is not a willpower problem. It is a system problem.

The service businesses beating you are not working harder. Their system answers in seconds while they work. A chat box on the site. A text that fires when a call is missed. Campaigns that follow up on their own.

What answering first looks like

  • A chat box on your site that grabs the lead the moment they are interested.
  • Missed call text back so a missed call turns into a live text chat in seconds.
  • Follow up that runs itself so the person who wants to think about it still hears from you.

Put those together and you answer first every time. And you never touch your phone.
