The Product

What You Get

What exactly do I get for $199/month?
+
The entire Core Product: a custom professional website with chat widget and lead forms, a booking system with confirmations and reminders, full CRM, 5-star Google review automation, missed call text back, and the mobile app that ties it all together. One price, everything included.
What is HighLevel and do I need an account already?
+
HighLevel is the platform that powers your CRM, automations, and communications. We set everything up inside your own account — so you own your data and your system. No tech skills or existing account needed on your end.
How does the booking system help me make more money?
+
It auto-confirms every appointment, sends reminders before, and follows up after. Fewer no-shows means more completed jobs on your calendar — and every completed appointment automatically triggers a review request that builds your reputation.
What happens when I miss a call?
+
The system instantly texts the caller back, starts the conversation, and saves them as a lead in your CRM. You never lose a lead just because you were on a job, in a meeting, or with another customer.
Do I really get everything in one app?
+
Yes. Leads, chats, bookings, and reviews all consolidate into a single mobile app. No switching between five different tools — open one app and run your business from your phone.
Working With Us

Setup, Support & Terms

Do I need to be tech-savvy?
+
Not at all. We build everything, configure everything, and show you exactly how to use it. If you can send a text message, you can run this system.
How long until my system is live?
+
Most businesses have a live website and active automations within 2–3 weeks of their first call. We move fast without cutting corners.
Is there a contract or lock-in?
+
No lock-in contracts. It's month to month — we earn your business every month. Cancel anytime, no cancellation fees, no questions asked.
What happens on the free call?
+
A quick, no-pressure conversation — about 30 minutes. We learn your business, show you the system working, and map out exactly what you'd need. If it's not the right fit, we'll tell you honestly.
Do you only work with businesses near you?
+
No — everything we do is remote-friendly. We work with small businesses all across the US.
What if I already have a website?
+
Great — we'll take a look at it on the free call. In most cases we rebuild it inside your system so your chat widget, forms, bookings, and CRM all connect seamlessly, with no extra charge beyond your flat monthly price.
