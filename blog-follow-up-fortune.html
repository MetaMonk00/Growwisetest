The Fortune Is In The Follow-Up: Turning 'Maybe Later' Into Booked Jobs

4 min read · GrowWise Team

Every owner knows this one. A good lead asks smart questions. They say they need to talk it over with their spouse. Then they vanish. You did not lose that job to another shop. You lost it to silence.

Most people are not ready to buy the first time they call. Research on lead response shows timing changes everything. And timing does not stop after your first reply. The shop that stays politely in view while they decide usually gets the yes.

Why you do not follow up

Following up by hand is awkward and never ends. You do not want to bug anyone. You cannot remember who is due for a check in. And you have real work to do. So it happens once, maybe. Then never again. That is not a flaw in you. It is an impossible job for a person running a shop.

What automatic follow up looks like

  • Day 0: The lead comes in. They get an instant reply and the chat starts.
  • Day 2: A friendly nudge. "Hey, just checking in. Still want that quote?"
  • Day 7: Something useful. "Our schedule for next week is filling up. Want me to hold you a spot?"
  • After that: A check in now and then until they book or say stop.
Every message sounds like you and comes from your number. It stops the second they book. Your customer sees a shop that cares. You see jobs show up from leads you forgot about.
