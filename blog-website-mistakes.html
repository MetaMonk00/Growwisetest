7 Website Mistakes Costing Local Businesses Customers

5 min read · GrowWise Team

Most local websites were not built wrong on purpose. They just fell behind. Here are the seven mistakes we see most, and what each one costs you.

1. It is slow

People do not wait. Google found that folks leave more and more with every extra second a page takes. If your site takes five seconds, a chunk of your customers never see it at all.

2. It is not built for phones

Most local searches happen on a phone. A desktop-only site with tiny text says "we have not touched this in years." Stanford research says people judge your whole shop on signals like that.

3. No clear next step

If someone has to figure out what to do next, they will not. Every page needs one clear button. Book a call.

4. There is no way to reach you fast

"Call us during business hours" cuts out everyone browsing at night. Which is most people. A chat box and a text back system catch those folks instead.

5. The site never talks back

No chat. No quick reply. Just words sitting there. A chat box starts the talk while they are still interested and sends the lead to your inbox.

6. Your reviews are hidden

You worked hard for those five star reviews. BrightLocal research shows reviews are a big part of how people choose. Put them on your site where they can close deals.

7. Leads go nowhere

The worst mistake is the one you cannot see. A form that lands in an inbox nobody checks. Every lead should go to one inbox, get an instant reply, and get followed up on. Otherwise you paid for a site that fills your junk folder.

None of these are design problems. They are system problems. Fixing them is exactly what a done-for-you system is for.

  • Stanford Web Credibility Research
