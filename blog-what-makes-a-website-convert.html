What Makes A Business Website Actually Generate Leads

5 min read · GrowWise Team

Plenty of shops have a website that does nothing. It looks nice. It has photos. And it brings in zero calls. That is not a website. It is a flyer nobody asked for.

A site that actually brings in work is built differently. Here is what matters.

1. Speed comes first

Google studied this with SOASTA. When a page goes from one second to three seconds, people leave about a third more often. And it gets worse from there. A slow site loses the lead before it gets a chance.

2. Phones, always

Most local searches happen on a phone. Often in a hurry. Nobody types "plumber near me" at 9 at night for fun. If your site makes them pinch and zoom to find your info, they are gone.

3. One clear next step

Every page should push one action. Book a call. Not "follow us." Not a wall of text. Just a button they cannot miss. Confused people leave. Guided people book.

4. Talk to them right away

A chat box changes everything. Instead of hoping someone fills out a form, your site starts the talk. They type one line and your system takes it from there. Into your inbox. Into a text chat. Into a booked job.

5. Proof everywhere

Reviews. Before and after photos. Real results. BrightLocal research keeps showing that reviews drive who people call. So your best ones belong on your site doing work, not buried on page three of Google.

Design gets someone to look. Systems turn that look into a name, a number, and a job on your calendar.

Where This Comes From

  • Google and SOASTA Mobile Page Speed Study
  • BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey
  • Stanford Web Credibility Research
