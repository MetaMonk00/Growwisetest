Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What We Collect

You give us info when you book a call or fill out a form. That is things like your name, email, phone number, and business name. We only ask for what we need to help you.

How We Use It

We use it to answer you. To set up and run your call. And to send you things you asked for. We do not sell your info to anyone.

Text Messages

If you give us your number, you agree we can text you about your question or your service. Message and data rates may apply. Text STOP anytime to opt out.

Other Companies We Use

We run our system on trusted software like HighLevel. They handle some of your info so we can send messages and keep your contacts in order.

Cookies

Our site may use small files called cookies. They help the site work right and help us see what pages people visit. You can turn them off in your browser.

Your Choices

You can ask to see your info. You can ask us to fix it. You can ask us to delete it. Just reach out through our contact page and we will take care of it.
