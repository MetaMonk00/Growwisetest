The Fortune Is In The Follow-Up: Turning 'Maybe Later' Into Booked Jobs

4 min read · GrowWise Team

Here's a pattern every business owner knows: a promising lead asks great questions, says "let me talk to my spouse and get back to you"... and then vanishes. You didn't lose the job to a competitor. You lost it to silence.

Most leads aren't ready to buy the moment they first reach out — research on lead response (like the Harvard Business Review study below) shows timing dramatically affects outcomes, and timing doesn't end with the first reply. The business that stays gently present while the customer decides is usually the one that gets the yes.

Why you don't follow up (and shouldn't have to)

Following up manually is awkward and endless. You don't want to pester anyone, you can't remember who's due for a check-in, and you have actual work to do. So follow-up happens once, maybe — then never again. That's not a character flaw; it's an impossible job for a human with a business to run.

What automatic follow-up looks like

  • Day 0: Lead comes in — instant reply, conversation starts, lead saved to the CRM.
  • Day 2: A friendly nudge: "Hey, just checking in — still want that quote?"
  • Day 7: A little value: "Heads up, our schedule for next week is filling — want me to hold you a spot?"
  • Ongoing: Occasional check-ins until they book or opt out — polite, spaced out, automatic.
Every message sounds like you, comes from your number, and stops the moment they book. The customer experiences a business that cares. You experience jobs appearing on your calendar from leads you'd forgotten existed.
