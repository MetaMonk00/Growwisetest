Our Service

GrowWise provides done-for-you marketing systems for small businesses — including website design, review funnel setup, missed-call text-back, re-marketing and database reactivation campaigns, and social/review automation — delivered as a monthly subscription.

Billing

The service is billed monthly at the rate agreed at signup. There are no long-term contracts; the subscription is month to month.

Cancellation

You may cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future billing; the current billing period is not prorated. Your data and accounts built inside your own HighLevel account remain yours.

Acceptable Use

You agree to use the systems we build lawfully, including complying with messaging consent rules when contacting your own customers.

Questions

Questions about these terms? Reach us any time via the contact page.
