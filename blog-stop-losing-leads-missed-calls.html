Missed Calls Are Costing You Jobs — Here's The Fix

3 min read · GrowWise Team

You are on a roof. Or under a sink. Or talking to a customer. Your phone buzzes. An hour later you check it. There is a voicemail. Someone wanted a quote.

You call back. No answer. You already know what happened. They called the next shop on the list. Research on lead response backs this up. The odds of reaching someone drop fast after just a few minutes.

What a missed call really costs

A missed call is not a small thing. It is a customer holding out money while you are too busy to take it. Say your average job is $400 and you miss five new calls a week. That is thousands of dollars a month walking to your competition. And you never even know it happened.

Voicemail is where leads die

Almost nobody leaves a voicemail now. Even fewer wait for a call back. When you do not pick up, people think you are too busy for them. Then they move on in minutes. You cannot fix that by trying harder. You are busy because you are good at your job.

The fix is a text

The second the call goes unanswered, your system sends a text. Something like: "Hey! Sorry we missed your call. How can we help?"

  • They get an answer right away. So you are still first.
  • The talk moves to text, which they can do from anywhere.
  • They get saved as a contact with the whole chat attached.
  • You reply when you are free and book the job.
Your customer sees a shop that answers in seconds. You get to finish your work in peace. Both things are finally true at once.
