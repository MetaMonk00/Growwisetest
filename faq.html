The Product

What You Get

What exactly do I get for $199/month?
+
You get all of it. A website built for you with a chat box and quote forms. The 5-star review funnel. Missed call text back. Re-marketing campaigns. Reviews turned into posts. AI review replies. Database reactivation. And the app that works as your CRM and inbox. One price, everything included.
What is HighLevel and do I need an account already?
+
HighLevel is the software behind your CRM, inbox, and campaigns. We set it all up inside your own account, so you own your stuff. You do not need tech skills or an account already.
Do you really not filter out bad reviews?
+
Right, and we never will. Asking only the happy people is called review gating. It breaks FTC rules and Google rules, and it can get your page punished. Every one of your customers gets the same honest ask. You still end up with more reviews, because we fix the asking, not the score.
What happens when I miss a call?
+
The system instantly texts the caller back, starts the conversation, and saves them as a contact with the whole thread attached. You never lose a lead just because you were on a job, in a meeting, or with another customer.
Do I really get everything in one app?
+
Yes, and the app is your CRM too. Every customer and their whole history live there. Calls, texts, website chats, social messages, reviews, and campaigns land in one inbox. One thread per person. No jumping between five tools.
Working With Us

Setup, Support & Terms

Do I need to be tech-savvy?
+
Nope. We build it all and set it all up. Then we show you how to use it. If you can send a text, you can run this.
How long until my system is live?
+
Most service businesses are live in 2 to 3 weeks. We move fast, but we do not cut corners.
Is there a contract or lock-in?
+
No contracts. It is month to month, so we earn your business every month. Cancel anytime. No fees and no questions.
What happens on the free call?
+
A short call, about 30 minutes, with no pressure. We learn about your business, show you the system working, and plan what you need. If it is not a good fit, we will say so.
Do you only work with businesses near you?
+
No. We work online, so we help service businesses all over the US.
What if I already have a website?
+
Great. We will look at it on the free call. Most of the time we rebuild it inside your system so the chat box, forms, reviews, and campaigns all work together. No extra charge.
