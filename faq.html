The Product

What You Get

What exactly do I get for $199/month?
+
The entire system: a custom professional website with chat widget and lead capture, the 5-star review funnel, missed call text back, re-marketing campaigns, reviews turned into social posts, AI review responses, database reactivation campaigns, and the mobile app with one unified inbox. One price, everything included.
What is HighLevel and do I need an account already?
+
HighLevel is the platform that powers your inbox, automations, and campaigns. We set everything up inside your own account — so you own your data and your system. No tech skills or existing account needed on your end.
Do you really not filter out bad reviews?
+
Correct, and we never will. Screening customers first and only inviting the happy ones — review gating — breaks FTC rules and Google's review policies, and it can get your profile penalized. Every one of your customers gets the same honest invitation. You end up with more reviews because we fix the asking, not the scoring.
What happens when I miss a call?
+
The system instantly texts the caller back, starts the conversation, and saves them as a contact with the whole thread attached. You never lose a lead just because you were on a job, in a meeting, or with another customer.
Do I really get everything in one app?
+
Yes. Calls, texts, website chats, social messages, reviews, and campaigns all land in one unified inbox — one thread per customer. No switching between five different tools.
Working With Us

Setup, Support & Terms

Do I need to be tech-savvy?
+
Not at all. We build everything, configure everything, and show you exactly how to use it. If you can send a text message, you can run this system.
How long until my system is live?
+
Most businesses have a live website and active automations within 2–3 weeks of their first call. We move fast without cutting corners.
Is there a contract or lock-in?
+
No lock-in contracts. It's month to month — we earn your business every month. Cancel anytime, no cancellation fees, no questions asked.
What happens on the free call?
+
A quick, no-pressure conversation — about 30 minutes. We learn your business, show you the system working, and map out exactly what you'd need. If it's not the right fit, we'll tell you honestly.
Do you only work with businesses near you?
+
No — everything we do is remote-friendly. We work with small businesses all across the US.
What if I already have a website?
+
Great — we'll take a look at it on the free call. In most cases we rebuild it inside your system so your chat widget, forms, review funnel, and campaigns all connect seamlessly, with no extra charge beyond your flat monthly price.
