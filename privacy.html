Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

When you contact us, book a call, or use our forms, we collect the information you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, and business details — so we can respond to you and provide our services.

How We Use It

We use your information to respond to inquiries, schedule and confirm calls, provide and improve our services, and send communications you’ve requested. We do not sell your personal information.

Text Messaging

By providing your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from us related to your inquiry or service (like booking confirmations and reminders). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Third-Party Services

Our systems are powered by trusted platforms (such as HighLevel) that process data on our behalf to deliver scheduling, messaging, and CRM functionality.

Your Choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at (850) 123-4567 or through our contact page.
