Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use the system?
+
Not at all. We build everything, configure everything, and show you exactly how to use it. If you can send a text message, you can run this system.
How long does it take to get everything running?
+
Most businesses have a live website and active automations within 2–3 weeks of their first call. We move fast because your business can't afford to wait.
What is HighLevel and do I need to learn it all?
+
HighLevel is the platform that powers your CRM, automations, and communications. We set it up completely inside your own account and show you exactly the parts you use day-to-day. Nothing overwhelming.
What if I want to cancel?
+
Month-to-month means month-to-month. No contracts, no lock-in. If it's not working for you, cancel anytime. We'd rather earn your business every month than trap it.
Do you only work with local businesses near you?
+
No — everything we do is remote-friendly. We work with small businesses all across the US.
