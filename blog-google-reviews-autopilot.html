How To Get 5-Star Google Reviews On Autopilot

4 min read · GrowWise Team

When did a happy customer last leave you a review on their own? Right. Almost never.

They mean to. Then dinner happens. Life happens. And the review never does.

Meanwhile reviews are how people pick. BrightLocal studies people every year. They keep finding the same thing. Most folks read reviews before they hire a local service business. And your star count and how fresh the reviews are both change who they call.

Your work is fine. The asking is the problem.

Most service businesses never ask. Or they ask in a weird way. Or they ask three weeks late. The window is short. You want to ask right after the job, while your customer still feels good about it. Miss that window and the moment is gone.

How to make it run itself

  • The job gets marked done in your system.
  • A few minutes later your customer gets a friendly text with a link to your review page.
  • One tap. Thirty seconds. Review posted.
Do the math. Say you finish 40 jobs a month. If just 1 in 5 people leaves a review, that is 8 new reviews a month. Almost 100 a year. And you never ask anyone face to face.

Reviews stack up

More reviews means more trust. More trust means more calls. Reviews also help you show up higher on the map. So the same system builds your name and your spot in search at the same time.

One rule though. Ask everyone. Never only ask the happy ones. That is called review gating and it is against the law.

Where This Comes From

  • BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey
  • FTC Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials
