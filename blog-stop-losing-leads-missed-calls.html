Missed Calls Are Costing You Jobs — Here's The Fix

3 min read · GrowWise Team

You're on a roof, elbow-deep in a repair, or mid-conversation with a paying customer. Your phone buzzes. By the time you check it — an hour later — there's a voicemail: someone wanted a quote.

You call back. No answer. You know exactly what happened: they already called the next company on the list. Research on lead response backs this up — the odds of connecting with a prospect drop dramatically within minutes (see the Harvard Business Review study below).

The real cost of a missed call

A missed call isn't a minor annoyance — it's a customer holding money out to you while you're too busy to take it. If your average job is worth $400 and you miss five new-customer calls a week, that's potentially thousands of dollars a month walking straight to your competitors.

Voicemail is where leads go to die

Few customers leave voicemails anymore — and fewer wait for a callback. Customers interpret an unanswered call as "too busy for me" and move on within minutes. You can't fix that by trying harder to answer; you're busy because you're good at your job.

The fix: missed call text back

  • The customer gets an instant text response — so you're still "first."
  • The conversation moves to text, which they can do from anywhere.
  • The lead is captured and saved in your CRM with the full conversation.
  • You reply when you're free — from one app — and book the job.
The customer experiences a business that responds in seconds, every time. You experience finishing your work in peace. Both things are finally true at once.
